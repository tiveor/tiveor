## 🎬 AI content studios

These are local pipelines that turn an idea into published video and music, with Claude as the engine.

- 🎞️ **altech-gen** 🔒: topic → script → TTS → Whisper subtitles → FFmpeg video, ready for YouTube and TikTok.
- 📓 **notebooklm-studio** 🔒: creates videos with Google NotebookLM, transcribes them with whisper.cpp and writes titles, chapters and hashtags with Claude.
- 📰 **news-studio** 🔒: video newscasts generated from RSS, rendered in 16:9 and 9:16, with per-platform publishing status.
- 🎼 **suno-composer** + **suno-studio** 🔒: song ideation from a lyrics corpus, then generation, download and rating of songs in Suno.
- 🎥 **videoclip-studio** 🔒: Suno songs → Remotion music videos, with word-level lyric alignment and beat analysis, built by a Claude Code agent on top of a style catalog.
- 🤖 **pepito** 🔒: a command center for CLI actions, jobs, schedules and YouTube/TikTok publishing.
- 🎓 **academy** 🔒: a course platform with two formats over the same content (long-form lessons and TikTok-style reels), plus a studio that turns PDFs, notes or links into a full course.
